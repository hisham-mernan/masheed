PART  1  
Executive  Summary  
1.1  Overview  
Masheed  is  a  vertically  integrated  technology  platform  built  specifically  for  the  Saudi  Arabian  
Awqaf
 
sector.
 
Unlike  generic  ERP  solutions  that  focus  primarily  on  accounting  or  property  management,  
Masheed
 
is
 
designed
 
around
 
the
 
regulatory,
 
operational,
 
governance,
 
and
 
investment
 
lifecycle
 
of
 
endowments.
 
The  platform  consists  of  two  tightly  integrated  products:  
1.  Masheed  ERP  
An  enterprise  operating  system  for  Awqaf  organizations  that  enables  them  to  manage  every  
aspect
 
of
 
their
 
operations,
 
including:
 
●  Waqf  registration  ●  Governance  ●  Financial  management  ●  Assets  ●  Beneficiaries  ●  Investments  ●  Documents  ●  Compliance  ●  AI-powered  recommendations  ●  Expert  consultations  
The  ERP  is  designed  to  ensure  that  every  operational  activity  aligns  with  the  conditions  of  the  
Waqif,
 
supports
 
regulatory
 
compliance,
 
and
 
promotes
 
long-term
 
sustainability.
 
 
2.  Waqf  by  Masheed  
A  regulated  digital  investment  marketplace  that  connects  qualified  Awqaf  with  investors.  
The  marketplace  enables  Awqaf  organizations  to  publish  investment  opportunities  after  passing  
structured
 
legal,
 
financial,
 
governance,
 
and
 
compliance
 
reviews.

Investors  can  then  evaluate,  compare,  and  invest  in  opportunities  through  a  transparent,  
data-rich
 
platform.

2  Product  Philosophy  
Masheed  is  not  an  accounting  system.  
It  is  not  a  property  management  system.  
It  is  not  a  CRM.  
It  is  not  an  investment  marketplace.  
It  is  not  a  document  management  system.  
Instead,  
Masheed  is  an  Operating  System  for  Awqaf .  
Every  module  exists  to  support  one  or  more  of  the  following  objectives:  
1.  Protect  the  Waqf.  2.  Preserve  the  Waqif's  conditions.  3.  Improve  governance.  4.  Ensure  regulatory  compliance.  5.  Increase  operational  efficiency.  6.  Enhance  transparency.  7.  Improve  investment  readiness.  8.  Increase  sustainability.  9.  Enable  informed  decision-making  through  AI.  10.  Maximize  the  long-term  social  and  financial  impact  of  the  Waqf.  
Every  feature  added  to  the  platform  must  support  at  least  one  of  these  objectives.

For  example:

This  engine  ensures  that  operational  decisions  remain  aligned  with  the  Waqif's  intent.  
 
2.9  AI  Waqif  Interpreter
